Exam format

  • 60 questions in 60 minutes, mostly single-answer multiple choice with a few multiple-response and scenario/sequencing items (performance-based style).
  • Scaled score reported on a 100–900 scale; a scaled score of 600 or higher is a passing result (approximate, for practice purposes only).
  • You can flag questions for review and jump between questions freely before submitting.
  • The exam auto-submits when the timer reaches zero.
  • Answers and explanations are shown only after you submit.
